Privacy

What happens on your phone — and what never leaves it.

No servers, no accounts, no analytics. Screenshot Brain is a local app with an optional bridge to Google's Gemini API that you control with your own key.

Stays on your device

  • Screenshot copies, OCR text and the search index
  • OCR — ML Kit runs fully offline
  • Duplicate, blur and value analysis
  • Collections, tags and chat history
  • Your Gemini key and PIN (stored locally)

Goes to Gemini — only when you ask

  • A downscaled screenshot, to generate titles, summaries and tags during import
  • Your question plus text excerpts of relevant screenshots for Ask Brain — never full-image dumps
  • Any screenshot you mark “Exclude from AI” is never sent
  • Requests use your own key and quota — the app has no server in between

Extra controls: biometric or PIN app lock, blur protection for sensitive screenshots, and a Trash with Undo so permanent deletion always requires an explicit second confirmation.

FAQ

Questions people actually ask.

Does Screenshot Brain upload my screenshots?

No. Your library lives in the app's local storage. The only time content leaves the phone is when AI features are on: a downscaled copy is sent to Google's Gemini API with your own key to generate titles and summaries, and Ask Brain sends text excerpts (not full images) of relevant screenshots. You can exclude any screenshot from AI, or use the whole app without a key at all.

Can it work offline?

Yes — the core is fully offline. OCR (ML Kit), search, collections, timeline, cleanup analysis, and the library all work without internet. AI features (titles, tags, Ask Brain) show a clear offline state and resume when you reconnect.

Does it automatically delete screenshots?

Never. Cleanup only suggests — every suggestion carries a reason and an estimated space saving. Deleting moves shots to Trash with Undo, and emptying the Trash requires an explicit confirmation. Your phone's gallery originals are never touched.

Is it free?

The app is free and open source. AI features use your own free-tier Google Gemini API key, so there is no subscription and no hidden cost.

Which Android versions are supported?

Android 8.0 (API 26) and above, including modern photo-permission handling on Android 13+ and partial gallery access on Android 14+.

How accurate is the OCR?

Screenshots are ideal OCR input — clean digital text. Screenshot Brain feeds ML Kit a sharp 1600px frame for maximum accuracy and keeps the recognized text searchable on-device.
